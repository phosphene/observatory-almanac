## Notes

- Area slugs are stable. Do not rename them without a migration plan — slugs appear in article frontmatter across all content files.
- The live observatory.wiki uses different capitalisation and spacing in its URLs (e.g. `/Charter_Schools`). The Almanac normalises to lowercase-hyphens.
- "Dig Labs" is a distinctive Observatory feature — deep investigative pieces on contested or underexplored topics.
