# Gardening

Home and community growing, soil health, and plant care.

---

## Community Gardening

Shared plots, neighborhood growing cooperatives, and collective food production.
Community gardens provide land access to residents who lack private outdoor space
and serve as hubs for food security, education, and neighborhood connection.

---

## Apartment Gardening

Container growing, balcony gardens, indoor herb and vegetable cultivation.
Apartment gardening adapts growing techniques for limited space — raised planters,
window boxes, grow lights, and vertical systems — making fresh food accessible
without a yard.

*Guides coming soon.*

---

## Backyard Farming

Small-scale food production on residential land — raised beds, fruit trees,
composting, and small livestock where permitted. Backyard farming integrates
growing, preserving, and soil-building into everyday home life.
