## Selection Criteria

Timeless myths, misconceptions, folk beliefs, and testable claims ONLY:
- Health, body, medicine, nutrition myths
- Folk wisdom, old wives tales, phenomenological claims
- Science myths and persistent cultural misconceptions
- Animal biology and behavior myths
- Psychology, cognition, consciousness claims

COVID events, novelty news, specific incidents, product warnings.
