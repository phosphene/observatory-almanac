# "The Moon Is Hollow / Rusting / Shrinking"


## 1. The Claim

**The Claim**: A cluster of persistent rumors holds that the Moon is physically peculiar in ways that mainstream science either ignores or suppresses:

**The Hollow Moon Hypothesis** posits that the Moon is hollow — either naturally or artificially — and that this hollowness explains why seismic data from Apollo missions returned unusually long-duration vibrations. In its most conspiratorial form, the Moon is an artificial alien spacecraft parked in orbit around Earth; in its more modest form, the Moon has a large hollow interior that compromises the standard planetary formation models.

**The Rusting Moon** claim holds that the Moon, which lacks liquid water and has an extremely thin atmosphere incapable of supporting oxidation chemistry, is nonetheless actively rusting — implying hidden water sources, suppressed geological processes, or simply that "scientists are wrong" about key lunar properties. Versions circulating in 2020–2021 appeared to draw on a real Nature Geoscience study as source material.

**The Shrinking Moon** asserts that the Moon is physically contracting and shrinking, supporting catastrophist or young-universe interpretations and occasionally invoked to suggest that the Moon's interior is "dying" in ways that threaten Earth. Some versions claim the Moon has shrunk measurably within recorded history; others project forward to suggest eventual catastrophic lunar collapse.

These three claims circulate independently but are frequently combined into a single "the Moon is weird and scientists are covering something up" narrative package, especially in YouTube conspiratorial astronomy content and alternative science forums.


## 2. What's Actually True

**Lunar Seismology and the "Hollow Moon" Misreading**: The Apollo missions did deploy seismometers on the lunar surface. Specifically, Apollo 12 (1969), Apollo 14, 15, 16, and 17 all placed seismometers; the network operated from 1969 to 1977. The seismometers recorded thousands of moonquakes of various categories: deep moonquakes (below 700 km), shallow moonquakes (within 100 km of the surface), thermal moonquakes, and impacts (Nakamura et al., 1982, "Apollo Seismic Experiment Final Summary," *Journal of Geophysical Research*, 87(S01), A117–A123).

The hollow Moon claim typically cites an observation by astronauts that the Moon "rang like a bell" when the ascent stage of the Apollo 12 lunar module was deliberately crashed into the surface as a seismic test. This event did produce an unusually long-duration seismic signal — approximately 55 minutes of measurable vibration. This is a documented fact.

What the claim gets wrong is the interpretation. Long seismic signal duration does not imply hollowness. It implies extremely low seismic attenuation — the lunar crust and mantle material dissipates seismic energy very slowly compared to Earth, because the Moon lacks the water-saturated sediments and liquid water in its interior that on Earth rapidly absorb and scatter seismic waves (Latham et al., 1970, "Seismic Data from Man-Made Impacts on the Moon," *Science*, 170(3958), 620–626). A dry, fractured, waterless rocky body will "ring" longer than a wet, unconsolidated one. This is completely consistent with models of the lunar interior and does not require hollowness to explain.

Direct modeling of the lunar interior through seismic tomography using the Apollo data has established a well-constrained model of the Moon's internal structure: a crust approximately 50 km thick (thinner on the nearside), a silicate mantle extending to approximately 1,500 km depth, a fluid outer core approximately 330 km in radius, and a solid inner core approximately 240 km in radius (Garcia et al., 2011, "Seismic Velocity Structure and Moonquake Locations Determined from a New Seismic Dataset," *Journal of Geophysical Research: Planets*, 116, E04003). A hollow Moon is structurally impossible within these constraints — the interior is occupied by well-characterized solid and molten material.

The artificial Moon hypothesis — that the Moon is an engineered spacecraft — is a variant of the hollow Moon claim that appeared prominently in a 1970 article in *Sputnik* magazine by Soviet scientists Vasin and Shcherbakov, who proposed that the Moon was a hollowed-out spacecraft placed in Earth orbit. This article was widely republished in Western alternative science communities but was never peer-reviewed, never cited in scientific literature, and contains no viable physical model — it is a speculative essay, not a scientific hypothesis. Vasin and Shcherbakov's credentials and the context of publication are never cited by those who promote the hypothesis; the claim circulates stripped of its origin as a sensationalistic popular-science magazine piece (Vasin & Shcherbakov, 1970, "Is the Moon the Creation of Alien Intelligence?" *Sputnik*, September 1970).

**The Rusting Moon — What the Study Actually Found**: In 2020, a study published in *Science Advances* reported detection of hematite — a form of iron oxide, specifically Fe₂O₃, commonly called rust — in remote sensing data from the Moon's poles, particularly at high latitudes on the nearside (Li et al., 2020, "Widespread Hematite at High Latitudes of the Moon," *Science Advances*, 6(36), eaba1940).

This finding was real, published in a peer-reviewed journal of the American Association for the Advancement of Science, and represented a genuine puzzle in lunar geochemistry. Hematite formation typically requires both oxygen and hydrogen (water or hydroxyl), and the Moon's surface environment is characterized by extremely low oxygen pressure and negligible liquid water — conditions that are formally incompatible with classical hematite formation pathways.

The proposed explanations in the Li et al. paper are scientifically cautious: the authors suggest that oxygen delivered by the solar wind from Earth's upper atmosphere (the ionosphere) via the geomagnetic tail — which the Moon passes through approximately monthly — could provide oxidant; trace water delivered by cometary impacts or retained in permanently shadowed craters at the poles could provide hydrogen. This is an unusual proposed mechanism and remains under active investigation.

Popular media coverage of this study used the framing "the Moon is rusting," which is technically defensible but profoundly misleading in terms of scale, mechanism, and implication. "The Moon is rusting" evokes active, ongoing corrosion of a metal structure. What the study actually found is hematite mineral grains in ancient regolith at the poles — not active rusting, not structural oxidation, not evidence of concealed water bodies. The hematite is a geological feature of ambiguous age and extent, detected via spectroscopic remote sensing and not directly sampled.

Critically, the claim that the Moon "has no water and no oxygen and therefore can't rust, proving scientists are lying" inverts both the finding and the scientific response. Scientists found what appears to be rust; they were the ones who flagged this as anomalous and proposed the unusual oxygen-transport mechanism as an explanation. There is no suppression; there is active, open, published scientific investigation.

**The Shrinking Moon — Thrust Faults and Contraction**: This claim has the most direct scientific basis of the three. The Moon is, in fact, slowly cooling and contracting. As its interior loses heat leftover from formation and from radioactive decay, the Moon's volume decreases slightly. This process is documented through surface features called lobate scarps — gently curved, cliff-like landforms that represent thrust faults created by crustal compression as the interior contracts (Watters et al., 2010, "Evidence of Recent Thrust Faulting on the Moon Revealed by the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera," *Science*, 329(5994), 936–940).

The Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) has mapped hundreds of these thrust faults across the lunar surface. Crosscutting relationships and the freshness of some scarps suggest that some thrust faulting may have occurred in the geologically recent past — possibly within the last 50 million years (a blink in geological time, though still long before any human civilization). More recent work has suggested that some thrust faulting may still be active (Watters et al., 2019, "Shallow Seismic Activity and Young Thrust Faults on the Nearside of the Moon," *Nature Geoscience*, 12, 411–417), consistent with some shallow moonquakes detected by Apollo seismometers.

The magnitude of lunar contraction is modest. Estimates from thrust fault analyses suggest the Moon's radius has decreased by approximately 100 meters over the past few hundred million years (Watters et al., 2010, ibid.). This is approximately 0.003% of the Moon's current radius of 1,737 km — a change that is scientifically detectable through careful geological mapping but utterly imperceptible on any human timescale.

The claim that the Moon is "shrinking" in a catastrophic or human-scale sense is not supported by any of this evidence. The Moon is losing radius at a rate of roughly 100 meters per several hundred million years — not per decade, not per century, not at a rate that affects Earth's tidal dynamics in any meaningful short-term sense.

Separately, the Moon is also **receding** from Earth — moving further away, not shrinking in absolute size — at approximately 3.8 centimeters per year due to tidal energy transfer (Chapront et al., 2002, "A New Determination of Lunar Orbital Parameters, Precession Constant, and Tidal Acceleration from LLR Measurements," *Astronomy and Astrophysics*, 387(2), 700–709). This is measured directly by lunar laser ranging experiments using retroreflectors placed on the lunar surface by Apollo astronauts. Recession is occasionally confused with shrinkage in popular accounts that conflate the Moon's orbital distance with its physical size.


## 3. Why People Believe This

Each of these three claims illustrates a distinct mechanism by which real scientific data is transformed into misinformation.

**The Hollow Moon claim** illustrates **anomaly entrepreneurship** — the systematic harvesting of genuinely anomalous or counterintuitive scientific findings and their presentation stripped of the explanatory context that resolves the anomaly. The long "ringing" duration of Apollo seismic signals is a genuine anomaly relative to intuitive expectation. The scientific literature explains it immediately and completely through attenuation physics. The hollow Moon narrative takes the anomaly without the explanation and presents it as unexplained, implying suppression or scientific failure. This is a specific cognitive exploit: audiences assess scientific credibility in part by whether scientists have candidates for explanatory gaps. Anomaly entrepreneurs manufacture the appearance of explanatory gaps where none exist by removing context.

A claim-specific mechanism here is **artificial origin exceptionalism**: the Moon is large, approximately spherical, orbits Earth in a nearly circular orbit with a precisely face-locked orientation, and has a radius that places its visual disk in near-perfect correspondence with the Sun's visual disk during solar eclipses. These precise coincidences are documented facts. The artificial origin narrative converts these coincidences into design evidence by invoking the general principle that unlikely-seeming precise results require intentional explanation. This ignores the fact that physical, gravitational, and tidal mechanisms produce the observed orbital regularities without requiring artifice — but the aesthetic feeling of "too precise to be natural" has significant intuitive force that precedes and overrides analytical assessment.

**The Rusting Moon claim** illustrates the **semiotic mismatch exploit** — a journalistic headline ("the Moon is rusting!") that accurately encodes a technical finding in a sensationalistic surface form, which is then received by different audiences in ways ranging from the correct technical understanding to catastrophist misreadings. The finding involves iron oxide particles in ancient polar regolith detected spectroscopically. The headline maps this onto the culturally available schema of a metal structure undergoing active corrosion. The mismatch is intrinsic to the translation of technical findings into natural-language headlines, and it generates a systematic downstream misinformation effect that the original paper's authors have no mechanism to correct.

The specific mechanism is **schema substitution** — the systematic replacement of a technical schema (spectroscopic detection of hematite mineral grains in ancient geochemical context) with a culturally familiar schema (corrosion of metal) triggered by lexical similarity. This exploit does not require any deliberate deception; it emerges from the structure of popular science communication (Nisbet & Mooney, 2007, "Framing Science," *Science*, 316(5821), 56).

**The Shrinking Moon claim** illustrates **scale collapse** — the removal of quantitative context from technically accurate claims. "The Moon is shrinking" is technically accurate. "The Moon is shrinking approximately 100 meters in radius over several hundred million years" is also technically accurate but conveys an entirely different order-of-magnitude implication. The scale collapse is cognitively powerful because the human nervous system is not equipped to intuitively represent geological timescales; "several hundred million years" does not function as a meaningful quantitative constraint in everyday cognition. Numbers at that scale are effectively unbounded to most people, which means that "the Moon is shrinking" and "the Moon is shrinking fast enough to matter" are not distinguishable through ordinary intuition. The quantitative context that makes the claim benign requires cognitive tools — geological time sense, unit analysis — that are not default human capabilities.


## 4. Why This Matters

The Moon is arguably the object about which more scientifically reliable, publicly accessible documentation exists than almost any other extraterrestrial body. The Apollo program produced hundreds of thousands of samples, hours of surface-recorded seismic data, retroreflectors enabling millimeter-precision orbital measurements, and an enormous body of peer-reviewed literature. The Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter has completed detailed high-resolution mapping of the entire lunar surface. The Moon is, in a real sense, the best-characterized body in the solar system after Earth itself.

The persistence of hollow Moon, rusting Moon, and shrinking Moon myths in this context is not a testimony to scientific ignorance. It is a testimony to the failure of scale and explainer infrastructure in popular science communication — and, more importantly, to the systematic effort by alternative science content creators to mine the real scientific literature for raw material that can be stripped of context and recombined into anomaly narratives.

The practical consequence for public scientific literacy is meaningful. Lunar mythology is a gateway drug for broader conspiratorial narratives about space science: if the Moon is hollow and scientists are hiding it, then NASA's general trustworthiness is compromised, which bleeds into vaccine science, climate science, evolutionary biology. The epistemic structure of "scientists found anomaly, failed to address it, alternative explanation required" is identical across domains (Lewandowsky et al., 2012, "Misinformation and Its Correction: Continued Influence and Successful Debiasing," *Psychological Science in the Public Interest*, 13(3), 106–131). 

Debunking lunar myths is therefore not narrowly about the Moon; it is about modeling the cognitive process of anomaly resolution — showing audiences that genuine scientific anomalies get genuine scientific explanations, and teaching the skill of searching for those explanations before accepting the "unexplained" framing.

For the Moon specifically, there is also an aesthetic dimension worth acknowledging: the Moon genuinely is strange and interesting without any mythological enhancement. The near-perfect alignment of solar and lunar disks during total eclipses, the synchronous rotation that continuously presents the same face to Earth, the origin of the Moon in a giant impact approximately 4.5 billion years ago — these are real facts that are as remarkable as anything the hollow Moon hypothesis offers, and they are *true*. The mythology does not make the Moon more interesting; it makes it less accurately understood.


## 5. The Actual Evidence

**On hollowness**: The Apollo seismic network produced a well-constrained model of the lunar interior that is inconsistent with a hollow Moon. The solid inner core, fluid outer core, and mantle structure are established through seismic tomography (Garcia et al., 2011, ibid.). The long seismic signal duration is accounted for by low attenuation in a dry, fractured silicate body (Latham et al., 1970, ibid.). The artificial Moon hypothesis has no peer-reviewed scientific support and traces to a popular magazine article with no scientific credibility.

**On rusting**: Hematite has been detected spectrally in lunar polar regions (Li et al., 2020, ibid.). This is a real finding. Its interpretation requires an unusual oxygen-transport mechanism via Earth's geomagnetic tail. There is no evidence of widespread active corrosion, hidden water bodies, or suppressed geological processes. The finding is the product of open scientific investigation, not its concealment.

**On shrinking**: The Moon is cooling and contracting by approximately 100 meters in radius over geological timescales (Watters et al., 2010, ibid.), producing measurable thrust faults detected by LRO. This contraction is not catastrophic, not human-scale, and not a threat to Earth. The recession of the Moon from Earth at 3.8 cm/year (Chapront et al., 2002, ibid.) is a separate, well-measured process distinct from physical contraction.

In all three cases, the scientific literature is consistent, extensively published, and openly accessible. There is no credible evidence for institutional suppression, deliberate concealment, or scientific failure to account for the observed phenomena.


## 6. EARC Assignment and Gap Analysis

**EARC Mode: R — Refuted (Claims Addressed by Established Scientific Literature)**

All three claims addressed in this entry are refuted by peer-reviewed scientific literature that is both technically robust and publicly accessible. The hollow Moon hypothesis is excluded by seismic tomography. The rusting Moon narrative is a scale-collapse misreading of a real but modest geochemical finding. The shrinking Moon catastrophism is a failure of quantitative contextualization for a real but geologically negligible process.

**Gap Category: Refuted-but-Recurring**

The gap this entry addresses is the gap between the existence of robust scientific refutation and its failure to circulate in the same communities that circulate the myths. All three claims are addressed in the primary scientific literature and in high-quality science communication (NASA archives, peer-reviewed review articles, LRO public data releases). None of them are mysteries or ongoing controversies in planetary science. Yet they circulate continuously in conspiratorial and alternative-science communities that are either unaware of or resistant to the refuting literature.

The practical media literacy implication is the anomaly audit: when a claim presents a scientific finding as "unexplained" or "impossible according to mainstream science," the first analytical step should be to ask whether the scientific literature has in fact addressed the supposed anomaly. In the three cases examined here, the answer is consistently yes — the anomaly has been addressed, the explanation is published, and the "unexplained remaining mystery" framing is false. The anomaly audit is not technically demanding; it requires only a search of accessible scientific databases, the skill of evaluating source quality, and a baseline understanding that scientific literature does not typically ignore striking anomalies in well-studied systems.

The Moon, as the most studied extraterrestrial body in human history, is precisely the worst candidate for long-running scientific concealment of major structural anomalies. The hollowness, rusting, and shrinkage claims all survive not because they have survived scientific scrutiny, but because they circulate in communities that have not applied scientific scrutiny to them.
