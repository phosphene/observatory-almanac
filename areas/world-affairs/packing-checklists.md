# Packing Checklists

*10 trip types, fully categorized. Adapt to your personal needs — these are starting points, not rigid rules.*

---

## How to Use These Lists

- **Check the weather** before packing clothing — lists assume average conditions for each trip type
- **Airline carry-on rules:** Liquids 3.4oz/100ml, 1 quart-sized bag per person (TSA standard)
- **The 1–2–3–4–5–6 rule for one-week trips:** 1 hat, 2 shoes, 3 bottoms, 4 tops, 5 socks, 6 undergarments
- **Roll, don't fold** to save space and reduce wrinkles
- Items marked **★** are often forgotten but critical

## 3. Backpacking (Multi-Day, Self-Sufficient)

### Clothing (Merino Wool / Technical Fabrics)
- [ ] 2 moisture-wicking base layer tops
- [ ] 1 insulating mid-layer (fleece)
- [ ] 1 waterproof rain jacket ★
- [ ] 2 hiking pants / convertible pants
- [ ] 4 moisture-wicking underwear
- [ ] 4 pairs wool or synthetic hiking socks
- [ ] Gaiters (terrain-dependent)
- [ ] Sun hat + warm hat
- [ ] Gloves (pack even in summer for elevation)
- [ ] Hiking boots (broken in BEFORE the trip) ★

### Shelter & Sleep
- [ ] Tent or hammock
- [ ] Sleeping bag (appropriate temperature rating)
- [ ] Sleeping pad / mat

### Navigation & Safety
- [ ] Topographic map of the area
- [ ] Compass
- [ ] GPS device or downloaded offline maps ★
- [ ] Whistle
- [ ] Emergency signaling mirror
- [ ] First aid kit (blister care, bandages, SAM splint, antibiotic ointment, allergy meds)
- [ ] Headlamp + extra batteries ★
- [ ] Multi-tool or knife
- [ ] Fire starter (lighter, waterproof matches) ★

### Food & Water
- [ ] Water filter or purification tablets ★
- [ ] Collapsible water bottles or hydration bladder (2L minimum)
- [ ] Camp stove + fuel canisters
- [ ] Pot + utensils
- [ ] 1.5–2 lbs food per day per person (high-calorie)
- [ ] Trash bag (pack it in, pack it out)
- [ ] Bear canister or hang bag (in bear country) ★

### Hygiene & Leave No Trace
- [ ] Biodegradable soap
- [ ] Hand sanitizer
- [ ] Trowel (cat holes for waste)
- [ ] Waste bags / wag bags if required
- [ ] Sunscreen + lip balm with SPF
- [ ] Insect repellent (DEET or picaridin)
- [ ] Toothbrush + toothpaste (small)

### Tech
- [ ] Satellite communicator (SPOT, Garmin inReach) ★
- [ ] Solar charger or extra batteries
- [ ] Phone (downloaded maps, emergency contacts)

## 10. Carry-On Only

*The art of packing everything in one bag (typically 22" × 14" × 9" / 56 × 36 × 23 cm)*

### Strategy
- Choose a versatile, neutral color palette
- Stick to 3–4 colors that mix and match
- Every item should work with 2+ others
- Wear your bulkiest items on travel days

### Clothing (7-Day Example)
- [ ] 3 tops (1 worn)
- [ ] 2 bottoms (1 worn)
- [ ] 1 versatile dress or multi-use piece
- [ ] 1 layer (cardigan / light jacket — worn)
- [ ] 5 underwear
- [ ] 5 pairs socks (thin, quick-dry)
- [ ] 2 pairs shoes maximum: walking + versatile ★

### Toiletries (TSA 3-1-1 Compliant)
- [ ] All liquids in 3.4oz/100ml containers ★
- [ ] Quart-sized clear zip-lock bag ★
- [ ] Solid alternatives: shampoo bar, solid deodorant ★
- [ ] Minimal makeup (multi-use products)
- [ ] Contact lens solution (travel size) ★

### Tech
- [ ] Cable organizer keeps bag tidy ★
- [ ] Universal adapter (compact)
- [ ] Laptop in easily accessible pocket (security)
- [ ] Earbuds + charger

### Documents
- [ ] All in slim document organizer
- [ ] Digital backups: email yourself scans

### Space-Saving Tips
- [ ] Packing cubes to compress clothes ★
- [ ] Stuff socks inside shoes
- [ ] Wear heaviest shoes and jacket on flight
- [ ] Laundry in-trip: sink wash or laundromat
- [ ] Hotel toiletries eliminate some liquids ★

## Universal Packing Reminders

**Always bring:**
- ★ Medications (never in checked luggage)
- ★ Phone charger and adapter
- ★ One change of clothes in carry-on if checking bags
- ★ Snacks for transit
- ★ Reusable water bottle (empty through security)
- ★ Anything truly irreplaceable

**The final check:**
1. Passport / ID
2. Phone + charger
3. Wallet + cards + cash
4. Keys
5. Medications

If you have these five, everything else is solvable.
