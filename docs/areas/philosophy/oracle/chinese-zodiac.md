## The Legend of the Great Race

The traditional origin story holds that the Jade Emperor summoned all animals to a race across a great river. The order of finish determined the zodiac sequence. The Rat, unable to swim strongly, cleverly rode the Ox's back â and at the last moment leapt forward to finish first. The loyal Ox placed second. The powerful Tiger came third. And so the order was set, each animal carrying the wisdom (and the flaws) of how they ran their race.

This legend is not merely charming. It encodes the philosophical heart of the system: character is not just talent but *how you use what you have*. The Rat's victory was cunning, not strength. The Ox's diligence was noble, even in being used. Even last place â the Pig, who stopped to eat and bathe along the way â is celebrated, not condemned. Every way of being has its season.
