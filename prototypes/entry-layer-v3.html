    On evaluation and scoring
    Any scores — including WCI — are produced by evaluating the actual content of your work. They cannot be generated from intake data alone. If you selected an evaluation route, results will arrive when that evaluation is complete. The score is not a verdict: it is a reading from a specific angle, against a defined rubric, at a specific moment. You will see exactly which dimensions were assessed and what each one measured.
